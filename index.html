        Plan ahead

        Greek dates that affect supply in 2027

        Around these dates, order 48 hours earlier than usual.

        • 15 MarClean Monday: public holiday, start of Lent. High demand for seafood.
        • 25 MarIndependence Day: public holiday.
        • 30 Apr – 3 MayOrthodox Easter: lamb sells out early. Short hours Friday and Saturday, closed Sunday and Monday.
        • 1 MayLabour Day: falls on Holy Saturday in 2027, so it is usually moved. Date to be confirmed.
        • 21 JunWhit Monday: banks closed, some suppliers closed.
        • 15 AugAssumption Day: peak season, ferries full, markets closed. Order by 12 August.
        • 28 OctOchi Day: public holiday.

          FAQ

          Good to know

          How far in advance should I order?

          On our next-day islands, order by 14:00 for delivery the next day. For a charter start, 3–5 days lets us source specialities and portion meat and fish. Elsewhere in Greece, allow 48–72 hours.

          Can I see prices before ordering?

          Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we send this week’s prices for what you need. No form, no sign-up.

          Is there a delivery fee or minimum order?

          Delivery is a separate line in your quote, so you see it before you pay.

          Do you deliver on Sundays and holidays?

          In season, yes, with notice. Supply is thinner on Greek holidays, so check the dates above and order early.

          What if a ferry is cancelled?

          We warn you as soon as a sailing is at risk and offer a plan B: an earlier ferry, a local supplier on your island, or a menu change.

          Do you supply villas and private chefs?

          Yes. We supply villa kitchens, private chefs and caterers as well as yachts.

          Can you find a product that isn't listed?

          Usually, yes. Add it to your list or send a photo on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, timing and price in the quote.
