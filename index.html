      FAQ

      Good to know

      How do prices work?

      Fresh-market prices change daily. Your quote prices every item and shows VAT and delivery. You pay only after you approve it.

      How do I pay?

      By card payment link or bank transfer, in euros. The order is confirmed once paid. We issue Greek e-invoices, reported to AADE myDATA.

      What if something isn't right?

      Tell us at delivery and we replace it or credit you.

      What if a ferry is cancelled?

      From June to September, especially in July and August, strong meltemi winds can cancel ferries. We watch the forecast and warn you the day before, with an alternative.

      Is there a delivery fee or minimum order?

      Delivery is a separate line in your quote, so you see it before you pay.

      Can you find a product that isn't listed?

      Usually, yes. Add it to your list or ask on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and price in the quote.
